This paper applies Extreme Value Theory to study the statistical behavior of extreme prime gaps and examines their relationship with Cramér’s conjecture. Using large-scale prime gap data, the analysis investigates the tail behavior of prime gap distributions and provides evidence of light-tailed behavior.
