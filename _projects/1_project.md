## 3D Topographic Modeling

**Gettysburg College · Research Assistant**  
*August 2026 – Present*

I am working on developing physical 3D topographic models of the South Mountain region.

The work involves geographic data, digital terrain modeling, and 3D printing. I've been exploring tools such as QGIS and TouchTerrain, preparing terrain models for printing, and testing prototypes to understand how elevation and terrain features translate into physical models.

The goal is to create durable, accessible models that can be used to explore the region's geography.
